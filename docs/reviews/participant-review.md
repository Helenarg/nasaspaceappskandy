# Participant journey and content review

Reviewed 9 October 2026 (Asia/Colombo), branch `space-apps-rebrand`, implementation commit `34fab26`. Reviewer: first-time participant, cautious parent/teacher, and potential community partner. Intent: make the site understandable, truthful and useful before adding more effects.

All ten content routes were inspected live in a separate browser tab; route sources and the organizer checklist were cross-checked. No valid form, agreement, account creation or backend write was submitted. These findings describe the local preview, not the deployed service.

## What to preserve

The homepage welcomes non-experts and multiple disciplines, separates hackathon/mentor/ambassador paths, credits space imagery and makes the local Kandy identity visible. The coherent dark blue/yellow palette and script-specific fonts offer a useful foundation. Strengthen those features with reliable event facts, real community evidence and a complete participation path.

## Reference lesson

The [official 2026 page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/) is the authority for global dates, registration sequence, team maximum and project-submission requirements. Its six-step journey is more useful for conversion than another decorative section. Corporate space sites supplied by the user can inform pacing and imagery; they cannot substantiate local NASA affiliation, prizes, schedules or certification claims.
