# NASA Space Apps Kandy 2026

NASA Space Apps Kandy is the local NASA Space Apps Challenge event in Kandy, Sri Lanka. The 2026 event takes place on November 14-15. Registration is open through the [official Kandy event page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/kandy/). The full local website is being prepared at [nasaspaceapps.lk](https://www.nasaspaceapps.lk/).

## Event facts

- **Event:** NASA Space Apps Kandy 2026
- **Location:** Kandy, Sri Lanka
- **Dates:** November 14-15, 2026
- **Registration:** [Official Kandy event page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/kandy/)
- **Kandy bootcamp:** EOE Pereira Theater, Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya. [Register](https://www.nasaspaceapps.lk/kandy-bootcamp)
- **Local contact:** [info@nasaspaceapps.lk](mailto:info@nasaspaceapps.lk)
- **Venue:** SLIIT Kandy UNI ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kTG7PyocgTkZ8WvK8))
